# Dimir Reanimator — how I want this deck played

**Draft.** These notes mix the simulator's rules with what my first two
recorded games show. I will keep refining them as more of my games get saved.
Where the two disagree I say so. If a spot comes up that these notes do not
cover, ask me — do not improvise.

This is the list with Superior Spider-Man, Bringer of the Last Gift, Beseech
the Mirror, Dark Matter Manipulator, Does Machines, Overlord of the Balemurk,
Terror of the Peaks, Ardyn, Valgavoth, Marang River Regent, Deceit, Theorix
Charm, Grim Bauble, and Bitter Triumph.

## Mulligans

Keep any seven with two or three lands and something to do on turn two — a
Dark Matter Manipulator, a Does Machines, or an Overlord I can cast for its
impending cost soon. No lands, five-plus lands, or nothing to cast before
turn four: ship it.

## The plan

Get Bringer of the Last Gift into the graveyard in the first few turns. On
turn four, cast Superior Spider-Man and have it copy Bringer. Bringer's
trigger wipes every board and then returns all creature cards from both
graveyards to play. One four-mana card rebuilds my whole graveyard as a
board. Terror of the Peaks turns that mass return into direct damage: every
creature that comes back is a Terror trigger.

One rule to remember: anything Spider-Man copies is a 4/4. It takes the name,
types, and abilities of the card it copies, but it keeps its own 4/4 body. So
a Spider-Man copying Bringer attacks for 4, not 6.

## When to go off

In my games I go off as soon as the combo is available: Spider-Man in hand,
Bringer in the graveyard, four mana — cast it. Waiting gives the opponent
time to find a counterspell or a graveyard sweeper.

The simulator is more patient: it only fires when the count below says the
combo kills within two turns. I have not settled this yet — when the count
says the combo clearly does nothing (an empty graveyard apart from Bringer),
holding is fine; when in doubt, go. The count, for reference:

- Damage now: each Terror already in play triggers for every creature that
  enters (including Spider-Man). Each Terror coming back from the graveyard
  triggers for the other creatures entering with it (not for Spider-Man, and
  not for itself). Add attacks from creatures that can already attack this
  turn. Demons attack right away if Ardyn is in play (he gives Demons haste).
- Damage later: add two more attack steps from the board the combo leaves
  behind. Spider-Man swings for 4. Creatures still on their impending
  counters don't count until the counters are gone. Dark Matter Manipulator
  gets +2 power for each 7 cards left in the graveyard.

The count already includes a kill this very turn, so there is never a reason
to hold back when it's lethal now.

Two more ways to use Spider-Man:

- Ardyn path: if Ardyn is in the graveyard with at least one other creature,
  Spider-Man can copy Ardyn. His trigger exiles the other creatures and makes
  a 5/5 Demon for each, and they have haste under Ardyn. This is fine even
  when it is not immediately lethal.
- Digging: with two Spider-Man in hand and a mill creature (Overlord or Dark
  Matter Manipulator) in the graveyard, it's fine to cast one Spider-Man as a
  copy of the mill creature to dig. Never spend the last Spider-Man this way.

## Discards

Whenever anything makes me discard (Does Machines, Coil and Catch, Bitter
Triumph), discard in this order:

1. Bringer of the Last Gift. Always first — it is the combo.
2. The other big creatures: Valgavoth, Terror of the Peaks, Marang River
   Regent, Deceit, Ardyn. The graveyard is where they want to be.
3. An extra Bitter Triumph, or an extra land (keep two in hand for land
   drops).
4. Anything that is not Superior Spider-Man.

Never discard Spider-Man while there is any other card in hand. It must stay
in hand — it is the one card that has to be cast.

## Bitter Triumph

Cast it when I need the removal, or as an instant-speed discard outlet when a
reanimation target is stuck in hand. Don't burn it for no reason.

Paying its extra cost: discard a reanimation target if one is in hand (that's
value, not a cost). Otherwise discard a blank. Only pay the 3 life when there
is nothing I'm willing to discard — and never pay life when I'm at 6 or less;
discard the least bad card instead. In a pinch it can even kill one of my own
spare creatures (a Dark Matter Manipulator) to put a creature in the
graveyard for the Ardyn path.

## Beseech the Mirror

Only cast it when the fetch does something:

- Combo target already in the graveyard, no Spider-Man in hand: get
  Spider-Man, and bargain whatever spare permanent is around to cast it free.
  (The simulator only free-casts when the combo kills within two turns; in my
  games I just go — see the combo timing note above.)
- Nothing in the graveyard yet but bargain fodder available: get Marang River
  Regent and cast its omen side, Coil and Catch, for free — draw 3, discard 1
  digs for the combo and bins a target in one card.
- Reanimation target stuck in hand: get Bitter Triumph (free with bargain) as
  the discard outlet.
- Otherwise: get Bringer to hand to set up later. Never bargain just for
  Bringer — at eight mana it can't be cast free anyway.

What to sacrifice to bargain, best first: an Overlord still on its impending
counters (the mass return brings it back as a full creature — an upgrade),
then a flipped Overlord, then Does Machines. Grim Bauble is an either/or with
its own surveil: if I have the mana to activate the Bauble this turn, keep it
and bargain Does Machines instead; only bargain the Bauble when its surveil
isn't affordable, or as a last resort.

## Deceit and Marang

Never hard-cast Deceit. Always evoke it for its two hybrid mana — it goes
straight to the graveyard, which is where I want a 5/5 for the mass return.
Pay with two blue when a bounce target is on board: the blue mode returns one
of my own permanents to hand, and the best bounces are a Does Machines (recast
it for another mill-draw-discard) or an Overlord still on its counters.
Against a control deck, two black is often better: the hand attack takes
their counterspell or sweeper. Otherwise pay with whatever is open.

Marang River Regent is mostly a graveyard card too. With spare mana and
nothing better to do, cast its omen side Coil and Catch (draw 3, discard 1) —
the discard order above applies, so it often bins a target. The card shuffles
back in, so nothing is lost. A reanimated Marang also bounces things when it
enters.

## Lands — plan for four mana on turn four

Turn four is the combo turn. Spider-Man costs four, so the land drops in the
first three turns should guarantee four untapped lands on turn four.

- While Spider-Man or Beseech is in hand, sequence for the combo turn: play
  the always-tapped lands (Undercity Sewers, Pit of Offerings, Starting Town
  after turn three) on turns where I don't need their mana, and save the
  untapped lands for when the mana matters.
- Starting Town is untapped on turns one to three and tapped after. Play it
  early, while it still comes in untapped.
- When the drop has to make mana right now, play an untapped land that lets
  me cast something — and spend the Starting Town first, since it would come
  in tapped later anyway.
- Prefer a land that adds a color I'm missing, and prefer the surveil land
  (Undercity Sewers) over a plain one when the choice is otherwise even.
- Watery Grave and Multiversal Passage: do not pay the 2 life unless I need
  the mana that same turn. Let them enter tapped on a turn with nothing to
  cast.
- Without a combo piece in hand, don't overthink it: play whatever casts the
  most right now and dig.
- One special case: Spider-Man in hand, a combo target in the graveyard, and
  exactly three untapped lands — play the untapped land FIRST, before casting
  anything else, so the combo can happen this turn.

Cavern of Souls: name Human first (it makes Spider-Man uncounterable — the
card that must resolve). If a Cavern is already on Human, name Demon for
Bringer, then Dragon for Terror and Marang, then Avatar for Overlord.

## Milling and surveil

When a mill or surveil lets me pick cards up or arrange them: reanimation
targets go to the graveyard, always — never fish Bringer or Terror back out.
Take back Spider-Man first if it shows up, then a blue land if I'm short on
blue, then any nonbasic land, then a basic, then a spare creature. On a
surveil, keep Spider-Man, lands, and mill spells on top; everything else goes
to the graveyard.

## Order of operations in a turn

When the combo is lethal, Spider-Man first — everything else can wait. If a
reanimation target is in hand, cast the discard outlet first (Bitter Triumph
or Does Machines). After that, mill spells before everything else: Overlord
(cast it for its cheap impending cost when possible), Dark Matter
Manipulator, Does Machines, Deceit, Theorix Charm (mill three, draw one).
Cheaper spells before expensive ones.

Spare mana at the end of the turn goes to, in order: Coil and Catch if Marang
is in hand; Grim Bauble's surveil (two and a black, sacrifice it); Does
Machines level two (one and a blue) when an artifact like a dead Bauble is in
the graveyard — it returns up to two artifacts to hand, Baubles first.

## From my own games (draft — refine as more games are saved)

These come from two of my Ranked games (both wins on the play, an older
version of the list). Two games are not enough to call them habits yet.

- Keep a 7-card hand with two or three lands and a turn-two enabler (Does
  Machines, or Overlord cast with impending). Both games started that way.
- Do not pay 2 life for Watery Grave or Multiversal Passage unless I need the
  mana that turn. Let them enter tapped on a turn with nothing to cast.
- When I discard, Bringer goes first, then the other big creatures (Marang,
  Overlord), then extra Bitter Triumphs I do not need.
- When the combo is ready, do it. In both games the opponent conceded the turn
  after Spider-Man copied Bringer.
- Beseech bargains whatever spare permanent is on the battlefield to cast
  Spider-Man free (Does Machines is fine when no Overlord is out).
- Against a control deck that attacks my hand or graveyard, evoke Deceit with
  two black for the hand attack: take their best card. It still puts Deceit in
  the graveyard for the mass return.
- If Spider-Man and Bringer are ready but I am one mana short, use the turn to
  dig for a land or another piece instead of passing.
